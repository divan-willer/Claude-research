**Gen AI accuracy.** Documented failures are real, but the evidence base is small. Frontiers retracted a February 2024 paper whose Midjourney figures showed a rat with grossly wrong anatomy and garbled labels [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/scientific-journal-frontiers-publishes-ai-generated-rat-with-gigantic-penis-in-worrying-incident) (snippet). A 2025 IEEE TVCG study interviewed 17 biomedical visualisation practitioners and researchers. It argues that the way these models are built limits their accuracy, "from imaginary (or fanciful) molecules to alien anatomy" [arXiv 2507.14494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14494) (snippet). On VideoScience-Bench, the two top video models, Sora-2 and Veo-3, scored about 64% and 58.7% on phenomenon congruency. Both still failed to show correct experimental procedure [arXiv 2512.02942](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02942v1) (snippet). I found no peer-reviewed test of Meshy, Tripo, Rodin or Hunyuan3D on anatomy. The Hunyuan3D 2.1 paper reports shape-similarity metrics, not anatomical accuracy [arXiv 2506.15442](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.15442) (snippet).
Inference (confidence: medium): through about 2028, generative video and 3D tools will not produce mechanism or anatomy frames that pass medical review without a human. They will keep spreading into previs, voice, translation and versioning.

**Where AI is already used.** One studio markets AI voice and lip-sync that turn one master video into 12+ Indian-language versions. ## Top 3 candidate gaps

### Gap 1: Human-authored, science-reviewed, rigged 3D biomedical asset library with provenance, delivered in offline and real-time formats

**Problem.** AI generators do not produce accurate biological 3D. Purely AI-made outputs cannot be owned in the US. Some pharma clients ban gen AI, and EU labelling now applies. So studios and agencies need base assets that are accurate, legally clean and documented. BioRender filled this need in 2D. I found no equivalent in rigged 3D, but my search for one was incomplete.

**Evidence**
- Practitioners say model architecture limits accuracy, "from imaginary (or fanciful) molecules to alien anatomy" ([arXiv 2507.14494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14494), snippet).
- No peer-reviewed anatomical test of Meshy, Tripo or Hunyuan3D was found; Hunyuan3D 2.1 is scored on similarity metrics ([arXiv 2506.15442](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.15442), snippet). A third-party paper reports Hunyuan3D 2.1 failing on a deer's legs ([arXiv 2603.21295](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.21295), snippet).
- The US Copyright Office says prompts alone are not authorship; human-made expressive input can be protected ([copyright.gov](https://copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html), snippet). Thaler cert denied 2 March 2026 ([Baker Donelson](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/supreme-court-denies-certiorari-in-thaler-v-perlmutter-ai-cannot-be-an-author-under-the-copyright-act), snippet).
- Some pharma clients prohibit gen AI ([Seven Bison](https://www.sevenbison.com/industries/healthcare-life-sciences), snippet). Fusion Animation keeps external pharma work AI-free and uses AI only for early concepts ([Fusion Animation](https://www.fusionanimation.co.uk/blog/ai-generated-content), snippet).
- EU Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026; Code of Practice finalised 10 June 2026 ([Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/taking-the-eu-ai-act-to-practice-the-final-transparency-code-of-practice), snippet; [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content), snippet).
- BioRender valued at USD 900M (Jan 2026) ([Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/en/newslanding/newsannouncements/2026/01/sidley-represents-biorender-in-investment-valuing-company-at-%24900-million), snippet). It added an AI figure agent, Leo ([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/biorender-launches-leo-ai-figure-builder-6ab9a1e8), snippet), and partnered with Anthropic on 23 Oct 2025 ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251023858531/en), snippet). No 3D product surfaced.
- Inference (confidence: medium): an AI-era pipeline needs an accurate base mesh or rig to condition on. A validated library gains value as AI tools spread. It does not lose it.

**Who pays.** Heads of production, CG supervisors and TDs at medical animation studios. Creative directors and producers at pharma/MedComms agencies. Content leads at med-ed publishers and simulation developers. Booth and experiential agencies that need real-time-ready assets.

**Rough price points.** Evidence thin; I gathered no asset-library or licence prices in this track. One secondary claim says a 3-minute MoA video can cost USD 6,000 ([VOKA](https://voka.io/medical-animation-trends/), snippet, 2026 article, unverified, likely a low-end figure). BioRender's valuation shows the 2D library model can scale. It is not a price point. Other tracks should supply licence pricing.

**Competitors / substitutes.** BioRender (2D, AI-enabled, no 3D found). AI 3D generators (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Hunyuan3D). Studios' own private libraries. Interactive anatomy platforms (BioDigital, Visible Body, Elsevier Complete Anatomy: not researched here). General 3D marketplaces (not researched here).

**Founder fit.** Very strong: hyper-real anatomical sculpting plus Maya rigging plus pipeline tooling is exactly what such a library needs.

**Confidence.** Medium-low. The technology and legal tailwinds are well evidenced. Demand, pricing and existing-library coverage were not tested in this track.

### Gap 2: Converting film-quality organic rigs to real-time formats (Maya deformer rigs to glTF/USD/WebGPU) for web, rep iPads, congress booths and XR

**Problem.** MoA and anatomy animation is built with offline-render rigs: custom deformers, simulations and blend shapes. These do not survive export to real-time formats. Meanwhile, WebGPU now ships in every major browser, booths want interactive 3D, and headset platforms keep changing. Real-time standards for rigged characters are not settled yet.

**Evidence**
- WebGPU is supported across Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; Safari 26 adds it on macOS, iOS, iPadOS and visionOS; Firefox Linux/Android support is still in progress ([web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/webgpu-supported-major-browsers), snippet).
- OpenUSD Core Spec 1.0 was announced 17 Dec 2025 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alliance-for-openusd-announces-core-specification-1-0-the-universal-language-for-building-3d-worlds-302644952.html), snippet). A new Characters, Motion and Interactivity working group is on the Core Spec 1.1 roadmap (March 2026) ([Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/aousd_prmarch2026), snippet).
- Khronos announced a glTF Gaussian-splatting release candidate on 3 Feb 2026, with ratification expected Q2 2026 ([Khronos](https://www.khronos.org/news/press/gltf-gaussian-splatting-press-release), snippet).
- Meta stopped selling Horizon managed services and commercial Quest SKUs on 20 Feb 2026, with support to 4 Jan 2030 ([Meta](https://forwork.meta.com/blog/an-update-on-meta-for-work/), snippet; [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/01/16/meta-stop-selling-quest-headsets-businesses-discontinue-multiple-vr-features/), snippet).

Random42 sells XR and holographic congress content ([Random42](https://random42.com/pharmaceutical-animation/conferences), snippet).
- Inference (confidence: medium): the durable channel for 2026-2028 is device-agnostic web/iPad 3D, with headsets as a booth novelty. That needs rigs baked into game-ready deformation (skinning, morph targets, vertex animation). This work is manual today.

**Who pays.** TDs and heads of production at medical animation studios moving into real time. Congress and experiential agencies (e.g. the type of Emota and Random42). Pharma omnichannel, brand and medical-affairs digital leads who commission interactive MoA. Med-ed XR developers (e.g. Lucid Reality Labs, which showed an intubation simulation on Android XR ([AWE](https://www.awexr.com/blog/lucid-reality-labs-galaxy-event-awe), snippet)).

**Rough price points.** Evidence thin; no sourced prices for conversion or real-time MoA work. One adjacent signal: Meta now gives Horizon managed services licences free of charge ([Meta](https://forwork.meta.com/blog/an-update-on-meta-for-work/), snippet). Device management is not where the money is.

**Competitors / substitutes.** Studios building directly in Unreal (Chasing Illusions says it uses Unreal for MoA visuals ([Chasing Illusions](https://www.chasingillusions.com/blog/top-10-medical-animation-use-cases), snippet; page attribution uncertain)). Booth/XR agencies. Engine vendors' own tools, NVIDIA Omniverse, and generic glTF exporters (none researched here). Manual re-rigging by game artists.

**Founder fit.** Strongest technical fit: Maya rigging, custom deformers and PySide pipeline tools are exactly the skills needed to build a baker or exporter for organic rigs.

**Confidence.** Low-medium. The platform shifts are well evidenced. Pharma budget for interactive versus linear content was not measured in this track.

### Gap 3: AI-assisted, traceable versioning and localisation of approved MoA and patient films (voice, on-screen 3D labels, short cuts), built for MLR re-review and EU labelling

**Problem.** Once a film is approved, every language and format version needs re-work and re-review. AI voice and lip-sync are already used, but they work on the flat video. They do not touch on-screen labels inside the 3D scene, and they leave no review trail. Pharma is adopting AI pre-review. Regulators are watching AI-made health content.

**Evidence**
- AI voice synthesis and lip-sync used to turn one master video into 12+ Indian-language versions; human medical and regulatory review kept ([Chasing Illusions](https://www.chasingillusions.com/blog/ai-animated-explainer-videos-pharma-brands-india), snippet).
- One studio estimates 6-8 weeks for a 1.5-2 minute MoA, rising to 12-16 weeks with multi-market localisation (snippet from a search returning [Motifmotion](https://motifmotion.com/services/scientific-animation) and [VOKA](https://voka.io/3d-medical-animation-services/pharmaceutical/) pages; exact page unconfirmed).
- Moderna first to go live with AI pre-review agents in Veeva PromoMats ([pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/market-access/ai-missing-link-fixing-mlr-or-reason-it-breaks), snippet).
- Modular-content tools that link to Veeva PromoMats are launching (Anthill Cloud, 2025) ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/anthill-cloud-transforming-pharma-marketing-with-ai-modular-content-and-omnichannel-engagement-302601309.html), snippet).
- FDA said in September 2025 it would monitor AI-generated health content ([Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/fda-begins-crackdown-on-direct-to-consumer-pharmaceutical-advertising), snippet). EU deployer labelling icons finalised 2026 ([Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/taking-the-eu-ai-act-to-practice-the-final-transparency-code-of-practice), snippet).
- Inference (confidence: low): re-rendering labels and timing from the source 3D scene, with a log for MLR, beats flat-video dubbing for pharma. I have no buyer evidence for this.

**Who pays.** Global brand managers and country/affiliate marketing leads at pharma companies. Medical affairs and patient-education teams. MedComms agencies. Patient-education publishers.

**Rough price points.** Evidence thin; no sourced localisation prices gathered. ZS reports 40-50% engagement lifts from gen-AI personalised variants in its own work ([ZS](https://www.zs.com/insights/generative-ai-pharma-content-personalization), snippet; vendor claim, not a price).

**Competitors / substitutes.** General AI dubbing and avatar tools (not researched here). Studios offering AI localisation (Chasing Illusions). Generative pharma video vendors (Quint Boa). Veeva PromoMats AI agents and modular-content platforms (Anthill Cloud). Language service providers.

**Founder fit.** Medium: combines his AI-product work (RAG, Claude tools) with 3D pipeline skills. It is less tied to his sculpting and rigging strengths.

**Confidence.** Low-medium. The pain is plausible and some evidence exists. The space is crowded by general AI tools, and platforms such as Veeva may absorb it.
